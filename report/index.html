Customer Segmentation & Retention

Which customers keep the business running, which ones are slipping away, and where should the next marketing pound go? An RFM segmentation of real transactions from a UK online gift retailer, built as a bronze → silver → gold SQL pipeline.

Dataset: UCI Online Retail, 1 Dec 2010 to 9 Dec 2011. Customers are scored on the day after the last order (10 Dec 2011).

    How it was built

    Plain SQL in three layers, run end to end with one command (python run.py). The same models port to Snowflake or BigQuery with minor syntax changes.

      Scoring rules

      Fixed business thresholds, so a score means the same thing at every snapshot and the 1,500 one-time buyers are never split across scores by an arbitrary tie-break

      ScoreRecency (days since last order)Frequency (orders)Monetary (sales, GBP)
      50–146 or more2,000+
      415–304–51,000–1,999
      331–903500–999
      291–1802250–499
      1over 1801under 250

      Segments come from the standard R × F grid (for example, R 5 and F 4–5 = Champions; R 1–2 and F 5 = Can't Lose). All 25 combinations map to a segment, so no customer lands in "other". The monetary score is kept for context and sorting.
